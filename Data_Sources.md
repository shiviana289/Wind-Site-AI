# Data Sources

| Layer | Source
|---|---|---|
| Wind Power Density | Global Wind Atlas (DTU)
| Transmission Lines | HIFLD Open Data via ArcGIS online
| Primary & Secondary Roads | US Census TIGER/Line 
| Land Use Land Cover | ESA Sentinel-2 (10m)
| Terrain Slope/DEM | USGS 3DEP 
| Existing Wind Turbines | USWTDB (USGS/LBNL/NREL)
